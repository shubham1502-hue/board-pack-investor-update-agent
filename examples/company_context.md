# Company Context

This is a fictional seed-stage AI SaaS company used for the demo.

Company:
ContextLayer AI

What it sells:
An AI knowledge workflow for support and success teams.

Stage:
Seed to Series A transition.

Current operating question:
Can the company keep growing MRR while improving activation and preserving enough runway to raise from a stronger position?

Audience:
Founders, board members, and current investors who need a concise monthly view of what changed, what is risky, and what decisions need to be made.
